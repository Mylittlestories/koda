## Tips for running a small beta

1. **Give a mission, not a menu.** "Try to reach the Sky Temple" works better than "test everything."
2. **Ask 3 questions** in your reply prompt:
   - What was the most fun thing?
   - What confused you or your kid?
   - Where did it feel boring or broken?
3. **Collect the built-in bug reports** — they include browser, screen, and steps, which makes
   fixing 10× faster.
4. **Aim for 10–20 testers**, not 200. You want signal, not volume.
5. **Say thank you publicly** — beta testers become your first community.
6. After the beta, point everyone to the public GitHub repo (issues tab) so feedback keeps flowing.
